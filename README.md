👋 About
Hi, I’m [Jiacheng “Karcen” Zheng (郑嘉诚)](https://karcen.github.io/). I’m an MPhil student in Applied Economics at the Ma Yinchu School of Economics, Tianjin University (2026–2029), where I apply data science and machine learning to economic research.

I began my data science journey at Dalian University of Technology (2019–2021), where I studied Chemical Engineering (GPA: 88/100; top 7.4%) and built a foundation in mathematical modeling and quantitative analysis. I then completed a B.Eng. in Bioengineering from Shandong University’s Marine College (2022–2026; GPA: 86.24/100; rank: 5/50; Outstanding Graduate). During this time, I also explored International Politics and International Economics and Trade at the School of Northeast Asian Studies, Shandong University (2022–2023), which sparked my passion for economic research.

My research spans economics, climate science, and AI. As a research assistant at the Center for Regional and Country-Specific Data Analytics, Shandong University (2023–2025; PI: Professor Yuan Li), I applied machine learning and network analysis to input–output economics and global value chains. In summer 2024, I collaborated with Professor Zhifu Mi at University College London (UCL), building citation-network databases for the IPCC Sixth Assessment Report. In early 2025, I contributed to generative AI alignment research at HKUST (2025), resulting in a paper accepted as a Spotlight at NeurIPS 2025 🎉—coauthored with researchers from Peking University.
